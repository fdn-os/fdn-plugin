Wait — I don't follow what you just said. Say that again: give me a little context first, use short
plain sentences (ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English), and use the words from `SPEC.md` for the
things in this app.

Drop the stack vocabulary entirely. No **D1**, **binding**, **worker**, **`_worker.js`**,
**hydration**, **StyleX**, **TanStack**, **bundle**, **finalize**, **dist/**, **push-builds**,
**provision**, **escalate**, **tenant**, **SSO gate**, **MCP**, **SPA**, **loader**, **migration**.

Say **the app's own database**, **the thing that runs on the server**, **the page's styling**, **a
copy of the finished app**, **putting it live**, **only people signed in to the company account can
see it**, **a change to how the data is stored**.

If a sentence still needs a term I have not heard before, explain it in the same breath — or lift
the sentence one level up until it does not need it at all. A number, a file name or a URL I can
click beats any amount of describing.
